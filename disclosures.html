Advertising and affiliate relationships

This page provides proposed disclosure language for World Skim; it does not assert that any unverified commercial relationship exists. Confirm sponsorships, affiliate compensation, lead-sharing arrangements, and other material relationships before launch.

When paid placements appear, label the placement clearly, such as “Advertisement” or “Paid sponsorship,” where readers see it. A footer link alone is not a substitute for a clear disclosure beside the relevant promotion.

Suggested language, only when accurate

  • Paid sponsorship: “Advertisement: [advertiser] paid for this placement.”
  • Affiliate link: “We may earn a commission if you buy through this link.” Use wording that matches the actual compensation trigger.
  • Paid referral: “We may be paid if you sign up through this link.”

Financial promotions

If the publication covers investments, general news is not personalized investment advice. Investing can result in loss. Paid securities promotions require specialist legal review for compensation, conflicts, holdings, and any securities-law disclosures. A generic affiliate or “not advice” disclaimer is not sufficient.

Editorial and third-party content

Use only content, images, quotes, logos, and fonts the operator is entitled to publish. Verify any independence, neutrality, fact-checking, or AI-use claims against the actual editorial workflow. Add accurate source credits and specific disclosures where needed.
