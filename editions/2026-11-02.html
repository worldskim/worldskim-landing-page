Iran Says It Could Reopen the Strait of Hormuz in Two Days — on One Condition

Iran's foreign ministry said it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if the U.S. accepts its demands, as Qatari mediators held talks in Tehran, The New York Times reports. A day earlier, Iran's IRGC navy said two oil tankers exploded after hitting mines in the strait. Iranian negotiators had left earlier talks in Doha, while a draft U.S. deal is circulating.

Sources: The New York Times · Reuters: tankers · Reuters: Doha talks

Fake Air-Raid Warnings Just Hit Phones in Ukraine and Russia

Hackers took over emergency-alert apps in both Ukraine and Russia and sent false air-raid and chemical or nuclear warnings, Reuters reports. Ukraine's air force confirmed its public alert app had been hacked. In a war where people rely on these alerts to know when to take cover, fake ones are dangerous.

Sources: Reuters

Canada Says a Deal to End Its Trade War With the U.S. Could Come Within Weeks

Prime Minister Mark Carney said a trade deal with the U.S. could come "in the coming weeks," Politico reports. That would end a trade fight between two of the world's most closely linked economies.

Sources: Politico

Bolsonaro's Son Left Brazil Through Argentina — and Now the Neighbors Are Feuding

Brazil summoned Argentina's chargé d'affaires after Sen. Flávio Bolsonaro left Brazil through Argentina, AP reports. President Trump says Bolsonaro can stay in the U.S. as long as he wants, while Secretary of State Marco Rubio denied that the U.S. helped him leave.

Sources: AP · Reuters

A Newborn Was Among 15 Killed When Myanmar's Military Hit a Maternity Clinic

Myanmar military strikes on a maternity clinic and a school killed at least 15 people, including a newborn, Reuters reports, citing local sources. Civilians keep paying the price in the country's long civil war.

Sources: Reuters
