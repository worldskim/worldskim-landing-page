Iran says it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz in two days. There's one condition

Iran says it could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within two days if Washington meets its terms. Those include lifting the U.S. naval blockade of Iranian ports, repealing sanctions and guaranteeing an end to strikes. It also wants a full regional ceasefire, including in Lebanon. Qatar and Pakistan are relaying the talks.

President Trump said a deal "looks like that's coming together very nicely," though Vice President JD Vance said no deadlines had been set. The strait remains dangerous. A day earlier, Iran's Revolutionary Guard navy said two tankers exploded after hitting mines while using an unauthorized route.

Fake air raid warnings just hit phones in Ukraine and Russia

Hackers took over Ukraine's Air Alert app late on Nov. 1 and sent false warnings of nuclear and chemical threats and street fighting, the Kyiv Independent reports. One message claimed Russia's Oreshnik missile was on its way. The alerts were cleared about an hour later.

Hours later, Russia's emergencies ministry said its own app was hit by a "massive cyberattack" that spread false information. Ukraine blamed Moscow, which named no culprit. The hacks came as a local truce in occupied Kherson ended, the longest ceasefire of the war so far.

Canada says a deal to end its trade war with the U.S. could come within weeks

Prime Minister Mark Carney said he had a "good conversation" with President Trump. He said teams will work "in the coming weeks" to deliver a deal. Talks collapsed in August, when the U.S. put 50% tariffs on about $20 billion of Canadian goods and Canada hit back.

Speaking in Iowa, Trump said the two sides have "the concept of a very good deal", the AP reports. He said it involves the Keystone XL pipeline and no tariffs for U.S. farmers. Relief on steel and autos has also been discussed.

World view

Israel's election chief said the results will stand after Likud claimed without evidence that Arab led parties "stole" the vote. Neither bloc has a majority in the 120 seat parliament.

Myanmar military strikes killed at least 15 people, including a week old baby, in attacks that hit a maternity ward in Rakhine state and a school in Loikaw, Reuters reports.

Israel extended its ban on Al Jazeera by 90 days, covering its broadcasts, offices and websites, and the network called for the ban to be lifted.

Need to know

A Learjet crashed in Greenacres, Florida, on Sunday, killing all nine people aboard after the crew reported ice and severe turbulence on a flight from Austin to the Bahamas.

The Justice Department asked a judge to consider sanctions against an immigrant rights group whose analysis challenged its list of suspected noncitizen voters in California.

An NBC poll found 40% of registered voters approve of President Trump, with 60% disapproving on the eve of the midterms, Al Jazeera's poll roundup shows.

Lindsay Clancy asked a Massachusetts judge to dismiss her murder charges after her first trial ended in a mistrial. The judge set another hearing for Nov. 16.

Money & markets

The ISM manufacturing index rose to 55.5 in October from 54.5, with production jumping to 59 as U.S. factories expanded for a 10th straight month.

Seven OPEC+ members led by Saudi Arabia and Russia agreed to keep December output at September levels and will meet again Nov. 29.

Ryanair's first half profit fell 15% to €2.15 billion as the price of its unhedged jet fuel roughly doubled, and it trimmed its traffic target to 214 million passengers.

Trajectory raised $45 million led by Sequoia for a platform that lets lawyers and doctors help retrain AI models without a research team.

Deep dive

Bolsonaro's son left Brazil through Argentina, and now the neighbors are feuding

Brazil summoned Argentina's top diplomat in the country after Sen. Flávio Bolsonaro slipped out through northern Argentina, Al Jazeera reports. Bolsonaro lost last month's presidential runoff to Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva. He had been ordered to surrender his passport and stay in Brazil. Instead he landed in Miami and called himself the legitimate president elect.

Brazilian investigators describe the trip as an "exfiltration" they call Operation Alba, Ynet reports, citing CNN Brasil. The family crossed into Argentina by van on Oct. 27. A private jet refueled in Ecuador and landed near Miami the next morning. A former Mossad officer, now a U.S. citizen, reportedly traveled with them from Brasília.

Bolsonaro is under investigation in a money laundering case tied to Banco Master, over an alleged payment to finance a film about his father. He denies wrongdoing and says he is being persecuted. Argentina's foreign minister denied receiving a formal request for explanations. President Javier Milei's spokesperson called the trip a rescue, and Bolsonaro thanked Milei without saying how he helped.

At a Halloween party at his Florida club, President Trump said Bolsonaro was treated "very shabbily." He added that Bolsonaro can stay "as long as he wants". Secretary of State Marco Rubio denied that Washington arranged the trip. The senator's father, former President Jair Bolsonaro, was sentenced to more than 27 years in prison over a coup plot.
