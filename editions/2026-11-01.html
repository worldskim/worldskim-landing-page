The AI Money Pit Just Gave Wall Street Its Worst Day Since June

On Friday, the S&P 500 fell 1.8% and the Nasdaq dropped 2.9%, the S&P's worst day since June. The fear: that the giant sums Big Tech is pouring into AI won't pay off soon enough. Intel plunged 28.6%, its worst drop since 1974, after a weak profit forecast, and Nvidia fell 4.2%. A day earlier, Alphabet had its worst day on record after reporting heavy cash burn tied to AI.

Sources: AP

At Least 69 Dead in Spain's Train Crash — and Officials Now Point at the Controllers

At least 69 people were killed in a train crash in Spain. Officials now say rail controllers approved the train's movement even though its collision course could be seen, The New York Times reports. The finding turns a tragedy into a question of who should have stopped it.

Sources: The New York Times

Riot Police Were Sent to Poland's Top Court

Judges backed by Poland's government tried to take control of the country's Constitutional Tribunal, and riot police were deployed. President Karol Nawrocki returned early from a trip to Senegal as the standoff unfolded. The clash is the latest round in a long fight over who controls Poland's courts.

Sources: The New York Times · AP

The World Just Admitted It's Off Track to Save Nature by 2030

The COP17 biodiversity summit in Yerevan, Armenia, wrapped up with countries reaffirming their promises to protect nature. But the world is still off track to hit its 2030 biodiversity targets, The New York Times reports, so the pledges now have to turn into action fast.

Sources: The New York Times

El Salvador Is Sending Up to 2,000 Troops to Fight Haiti's Gangs

El Salvador will deploy up to 2,000 troops to Haiti to take on the gangs that control much of the country, after President Trump approved the plan, Axios reports. It would be one of the largest foreign deployments yet in Haiti's long gang crisis.

Sources: Axios
